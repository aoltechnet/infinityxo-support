Frequently asked questions

How do I play?
Each player only ever holds 3 marks on the board. Place a 4th and your oldest mark fades away. Get 3 in a row to win — the board never fills up, so there are no draws. Open the app and choose "How to Play" from the menu for the full walkthrough.
How do I change the difficulty?
Open Settings from the main menu and choose Easy, Normal, or Hard for the computer opponent.
How do I switch visual themes?
Open Theme from the main menu to choose between Retro Arcade, Neon Synth, and Mono Arcade.
Can I play with a friend?
Yes — choose "2 Player" from the main menu and pass the watch back and forth.
Does the app collect any of my data?
No. Everything (settings and stats) stays on your watch. See the privacy policy for details.
