Frequently asked questions

How do I play?
In Infinity mode, each player only ever holds a few marks on the board. Place one more and your oldest mark fades away. Because the board never fills up, an Infinity game cannot end in a draw. Get a full line to win — across, down or diagonally. Open the app and choose "How To" (iPhone and iPad) or "How to Play" (Apple Watch) for the full walkthrough.
What is Classic mode?
Classic is plain tic‑tac‑toe with no vanishing marks, exactly as you remember it — which means a Classic game can end in a draw. Switch between Infinity and Classic under "Game Type": on iPhone and iPad it is right on the main screen, and on Apple Watch it is in Settings.
Can I change the board size?
Yes — 3×3, 4×4 and 5×5, in the same place as Game Type. On a 5×5 board you win with four in a row rather than five. Each game type and board size keeps its own win/loss record and streak.
How do I change the difficulty?
Choose Easy, Normal or Hard under "AI Difficulty" — on the main screen on iPhone and iPad, or in Settings on Apple Watch.
How do I switch visual themes?
Choose "Theme" from the main menu to switch between Retro Arcade, Neon Synth and Mono Arcade.
Can I play with a friend?
Yes — choose "2 Player" from the main menu and pass the device back and forth.
Do my stats sync between my iPhone and my watch?
No. Settings and stats are stored on each device separately, so your iPhone and your Apple Watch keep their own records.
Does the app collect any of my data?
No. Everything (settings and stats) stays on your device. See the privacy policy for details.
